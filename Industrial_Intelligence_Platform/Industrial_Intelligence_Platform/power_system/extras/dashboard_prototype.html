Smart Grid Operational Decision Pipeline

1 Smart Meter Telemetry

Captures 15-minute active power (kWh), reactive powers, and power factors over Modbus/MQTT.

2 Feature Engineering

Calculates cyclical trigonometric sine/cosine encodings, apparent power (kVAh), and NSM.

3 Extra Trees Classifier

Evaluates 500 decision trees in 2.1 ms with balanced weights to categorize the operational load regime.

4 Automated Grid Action

Triggers automated peak-shaving dispatch, power factor capacitor switching, and TOU billing.
